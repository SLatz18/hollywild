# Around the Next Corner

Optional neighborhood stories for Hollywild. These stories are independent of the main campaign and of each other. You can explore every public scene before starting them. Projects require the corresponding property to be owned; finishing an owned property's project has no further resource charge.

Use the neighborhood side desk in a location to open Stories, Moments, or Build. Start a track at its home desk, then follow its destination hint. Each interaction performs one step. The Side journal shows progress and the exact next destination; it does not start stories or perform their steps.

A finished mission pays once. If an ordinary job is active or your wallet or scrap storage cannot accept the reward, finish the job or make room, then retry at the final desk or Side journal. The pending result is preserved.

There are four three-mission story arcs, six short encounters, and four property projects: 22 missions and 94 authored steps. All mission rewards together total 700 Coins and 22 scrap. Buying the four properties is separate. No playtime estimate is asserted here.

## SPOILERS: complete walkthrough

The following routes include all puzzle answers and both later consequences of every story choice. A wrong puzzle answer displays a clue and leaves that step pending. Story choices are remembered within the side story and do not change the main campaign.

### Extremely Special Delivery

A courier named Fig has opened a delivery firm for things other firms politely leave behind. His first client is a ceremonial pudding. Its rider demands legroom.

**A Pudding With Standing**

Fig owns one cart, two mittens, and a business card longer than the cart. Help him deliver his first awkward promise.

1. Frostwick Depot: read Fig's pudding order at the side desk.
2. Candy Sorting: collect the blue bowl crate at the side desk.
3. Frostwick Depot: choose the pudding crate at the side desk. Answer: **BLUE**. The blue crate has a ring that holds the bowl upright. The flat red crate cannot steady it.
4. Candy Row: choose Fig's delivery style at the side desk. Choose either **FANFARE** or **CARD**.
   - FANFARE: Fig polishes a pocket horn. It plays one respectable note and one that resembles a wet boot.
   - CARD: Fig buys a pencil and a stack of cards. His first draft says: Your item has reached where you wanted it.
5. Candy Sorting: deliver the pudding at the side desk.
   - Later FANFARE reaction: The staff request the horn only after the sleeping apprentice wakes. Fig adds ASK FIRST to his fanfare policy.
   - Later CARD reaction: The staff pin the arrival card over the table. A quiet receipt earns a very loud round of applause.

Fig pays the first fee and crosses out WORLDWIDE on his card. Across Town is already a substantial claim.
Reward: 35 Coins, 1 scrap.

**The Office Goes Upstairs**

A Velvet tenant ordered a desk fern. The seller shipped an entire theatrical forest because the invoice said dramatic growth.

1. Velvet Heights: inspect the fern order at the side desk.
2. Velvet Rooftops: inspect the borrowed forest at the side desk.
3. Velvet Rooftops: select the reading-room fern at the side desk. Answer: **F4**. F4 has the square brass tag named on the order. The theatre pots must go back.
4. Candy Row: return the two theatre pots at the side desk.
5. Velvet Lodge: deliver F4 at the side desk.
   - Later FANFARE reaction: Fig asks before sounding his horn. The reader requests one tiny note outside the closed reading-room door.
   - Later CARD reaction: Fig leaves a card that names the pot. The reader uses its blank back to record watering duties.

One fern delivered, two forests returned. Fig adds a cargo count to every future order.
Reward: 43 Coins, 1 scrap.

**A Present for Nobody**

1. Parcel Palace: read the return label at the side desk.
2. Frostwick Archive: ask about the empty chair at the side desk.

Reward: 50 Coins, 1 scrap.

Completion: Fig's fitted crates are in service. Each successful ordinary delivery job pays 5 extra Coins.

### The View Is Negotiable

Nib has been offered a commission on a remarkably affordable rooftop cottage. The photograph contains a chimney, a sunset, and no apparent cottage.

**An Abundance of Sky**

The brochure promises detached living and unlimited headroom. Nib asks you to locate the walls before he orders a ribbon.

1. Velvet Lodge: read the cottage brochure at the side desk.
2. Velvet Rooftops: collect the lot markers at the side desk.
3. Velvet Rooftops: identify the advertised lot at the side desk. Answer: **LANDING**. The survey and the marker agree: Lot 8 is the stair landing. The hut stands on Lot 9.
4. Velvet Lodge: choose how to correct the brochure at the side desk. Choose either **FACTS** or **TOUR**.
   - FACTS: Nib prints a large lot number and a measured diagram. The sunset receives no square footage.
   - TOUR: Nib marks the photographer's kettle position. Visitors may inspect the illusion and then the real boundary.
5. Velvet Heights: show the correction at the side desk.
   - Later FACTS reaction: The buyer uses the facts card to compare a second address. Its plain little diagram becomes more useful than the glossy brochure.
   - Later TOUR reaction: The buyer attends the tour, then teaches a friend the kettle trick. Nib gets applause for explaining his own correction.

No purchase occurs. Nib returns the ribbon and receives a credit for a less ambitious length.
Reward: 36 Coins, 1 scrap.

2. Brassworks Foundry: collect the smooth bench boards at the side desk.
3. Candy Row: choose the bench surface at the side desk. Answer: **BOARDS**. The smooth boards replace the split seat. Gears have sharp teeth; ribbon cannot bear a sitter.
4. Candy Row: choose the restored corner's purpose at the side desk. Choose either **SHELF** or **BOARD**.
   - SHELF: A shelf gets two sturdy hooks and a sign: Take what helps. Leave it usable.
   - BOARD: A board gets a weather lip and chalk. Its first request is for someone to draw a better sun.
5. Frostwick: invite the block's residents at the side desk.
   - Later SHELF reaction: A neighbor offers clean mittens for the sharing shelf. Elm labels the sizes so nobody has to unfold every pair.
   - Later BOARD reaction: A neighbor writes LIFT TO MARKET beside their name. Another adds a reply and agrees the arrangement directly.

The bench seats three people and a bag of carrots. Elm refuses to count the carrots as a fourth resident.
Reward: 36 Coins, 1 scrap.

   - SOLO: Bea records your solo win and asks for a rematch with better crate knowledge.
